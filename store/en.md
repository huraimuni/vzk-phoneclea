# English (en-US)

## 앱 이름 (30자)
VZK PhoneClean: Junk App Check

## 간단한 설명 (72자)
Find, pause and remove ad-installed junk apps. Family can help remotely.

## 자세한 설명 (1447자)
Did an unknown app show up on your parents' phone? Apps installed from ad pop-ups or quietly in the background slow the phone down and flood it with ads. VZK PhoneClean finds them.

WHAT IT DOES
• Suspicious app check: analyses installed apps and explains in plain words which look risky or need caution, with the reasons.
• Pause or delete: stop a suspicious app or open the system uninstall screen. The app never deletes anything by itself.
• Instant new-app alerts: get the analysis the moment a new app is installed.
• Notification cleanup: clear piled-up notifications and turn off ad alerts.
• Ad and algorithm cleanup guide, plus an emergency guide for when the phone freezes.
• Scam check (Korean only): call checklist, suspicious text and link check, and a phone risk check.

FAMILY CAN HELP FROM AFAR (subscription)
• A child sees the parent's phone screen and shows where to tap. It connects only when the parent allows it.
• Link once, and next time request help without a code.
• Check report: with the parent's consent, a child is notified when a risky app is installed. Only the app name and the reason are sent.
• The parent being helped is always free. The helping child subscribes monthly.

PRIVACY
• App checks run on your phone only. Your installed-app list is never sent to a server.
• Signing in is optional (needed only for family features). No ads.

Note: results are guidance only. Please make the final decision yourself.
